## 5. Scenario-Based Demo & Practice Questions

### Question 1
In Oracle Planning Central Cloud, which collection type should an organization use when only specific transactional entities (such as Purchase Orders and Sales Orders) have changed since the last collection run?

A) Complete Refresh Collection
B) Targeted Collection
C) Archive Collection
D) Static Snapshot Collection

- **Correct Answer**: `B) Targeted Collection`
- **Technical Explanation**: Targeted Collections allow planners to select specific business entities to refresh without running a time-consuming Complete Refresh of all planning data.

### Question 2
How does Oracle Planning Central calculate time-phased safety stock levels when using statistical inventory planning?

A) By fixing the safety stock to a constant flat 10 units for all items
B) By analyzing demand variability, lead time variability, and the desired customer service level target
C) By multiplying total warehouse square footage by unit weight
D) By taking the average of last year's sales without lead time consideration

- **Correct Answer**: `B) By analyzing demand variability, lead time variability, and the desired customer service level target`
- **Technical Explanation**: Statistical safety stock calculations evaluate historical demand volatility, replenishment lead time volatility, and the configured service level percentage to protect against stockouts dynamically.
